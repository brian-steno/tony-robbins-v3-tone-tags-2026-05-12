Break the state pattern

This packet uses direct fixed-text TTS renders. It is not the LiveKit runtime smoke page.

Plain text target:

Right now the goal is not to solve your whole life in one move. The goal is to break the emotional pattern that has been running you. Stand up, change your physiology, breathe deeper, and make one decision that puts you back in motion. When people stay stuck, it is usually because they keep rehearsing the problem instead of conditioning a new state. You do not need more self-judgment. You need momentum, clarity, and one meaningful action completed today. Focus on what you can control in the next hour, not on everything that still feels unfinished.
